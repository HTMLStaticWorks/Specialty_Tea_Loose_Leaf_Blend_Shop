ABOUT US / OUR STORY

A quieter ritual, born from reverent soil.

Mist & Leaf was founded on an unhurried philosophy: that true tea is not a hurried commodity, but an intimate daily meditation. We travel to generational family-tended gardens in Assam, Uji, Shizuoka, and high-altitude mist valleys to curate expressive whole leaves.

Every single blend is hand-composed in our studio, free from synthetic extracts, bleached bags, or dust. We celebrate the natural geometry of the whole leaf and the meditative pause it creates in every cup.

OUR COMMITMENT

Why Choose Our Tea.

Four unwavering standards behind every leaf, harvest, and studio blend we offer.

01 / INTEGRITY

100% Whole Loose Leaf

We reject industrial tea dust, fannings, and bleached paper teabags. Intact, uncrushed leaves preserve delicate essential oils and re-infuse beautifully through 3 to 5 steepings.

02 / SOURCING

Direct Estate Partnerships

We work directly with multigenerational organic family gardens. Sourcing directly bypasses commercial auction houses and ensures growers receive over 3x fair-trade premiums.

03 / CRAFTSMANSHIP

Small-Batch Blending

Every herbal formulation and signature spice blend is weighed, mixed, and packaged by hand in our studio in micro-batches to guarantee peak aroma and freshness.

04 / RESPONSIBILITY

100% Compostable Packaging

Our pouches are made from certified plant-based films with zero microplastics, and our reusable ultraviolet tins protect precious aromatics while honoring our planet.
